ByteMind Risk Review Guidelines

Risk scoring combines signals from multiple verification modules.

Potential risk factors may include:
- OCR failure
- MRZ not detected
- MRZ parsing failure
- Document face unavailable
- Live photo face unavailable
- Multiple faces detected
- Possible document tampering
- Verification warnings

A higher risk score indicates that more verification concerns were detected by the configured rules.

Risk levels used by ByteMind are:
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

Low risk indicates that relatively few configured risk signals were detected.

Medium risk indicates that additional verification checks may be appropriate.

High risk indicates that manual review may be appropriate before approval.

Critical risk indicates that detailed manual investigation may be appropriate.

Risk scoring is a screening mechanism and does not by itself establish whether an identity document is genuine or fraudulent.

Risk factors should be displayed together with their impact and reason.

The risk engine should remain deterministic and separate from the RAG knowledge retrieval system.

RAG should provide supporting knowledge and explanations for detected findings rather than changing the numerical risk score.

When evidence is incomplete, the system should communicate the limitation rather than inventing missing information.
